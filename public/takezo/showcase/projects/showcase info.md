# Portfolio Projects

## 01. Crab

**Title:** Crab  
**Short Info:** A realistic organic 3D study that evolved into an advanced rigging project, combining anatomical research, detailed sculpting, and complex rig development.  
**Year:** 2025  
**Software Used:** Blender, Adobe Substance 3D Painter, ZBrush

**Main Info:**

Crab began as a personal initiative to achieve realism in organic 3D art. Drawing on personal research through Pinterest references and point-cloud data, I explored the crab's anatomy and appearance through digital sculpting and realistic texturing.

The project evolved into my most complex rigging undertaking to date. Its rig uses an extensive network of constraints, scripting, and nested parenting to support the crab's intricate anatomy and movement. It represents a milestone in my exploration of both organic realism and technical character rigging.

## 04. Project Zaman

**Title:** Project Zaman  
**Short Info:** An ongoing surreal desert-dystopian world-building project blending Persian aesthetics, psychological symbolism, retrofuturistic industrial design, and adaptable 3D character development.  
**Year:** 2024–2026 (Ongoing)  
**Software Used:** Blender

**Main Info:**

Project Zaman is a personal creative project centered on a surreal desert dystopia: an inner world formed in the mind of an intellectual, where symbolic storytelling offers a key to understanding the setting. Its core world is rooted in an early 20th-century aesthetic, but its standards have advanced ahead of their time through mysterious, forbidden methods of innovation.

Its visual direction brings together Persian aesthetics, liminal spaces, vintage industrial design, and steampunk, with inspiration from *Lies of P* and *Bloodborne*. My work includes character, machine, and environment concepts, alongside reusable male and female 3D base models designed to be morphed into different characters. The project continues to develop through both visual world-building and practical character production.

## 06. Bye Bye Gyro Parody

**Title:** Bye Bye Gyro Parody  
**Short Info:** An unfinished crossover parody concept inspired by *Steel Ball Run* and *Deadpool & Wolverine*, featuring a fully modeled and textured Gyro Zeppeli.  
**Year:** 2024  
**Software Used:** Blender, Adobe Substance 3D Painter, Adobe Photoshop

**Main Info:**

Bye Bye Gyro Parody started with an original crossover idea inspired by the snowy *Steel Ball Run* scene in which Johnny and Gyro later confront the Eleven Men. I imagined Gyro using the Saint's Corpse bones as weapons, drawing on the scene in *Deadpool & Wolverine* where Deadpool uses Wolverine's skeletal remains in combat.

I developed the concept and storyboard in Photoshop, then modeled Gyro entirely from scratch in Blender and textured him in Substance 3D Painter. I completed the character model and textures, but did not proceed to realize the full parody sequence.

## 11. Twisted Chess Game

**Title:** Twisted Chess Game  
**Short Info:** A sixth-semester university 3D chess project with a custom Monk piece, additional strategy mechanics, and a developed visual presentation.  
**Year:** 2025  
**Software Used:** Unity, Blender, Adobe Substance 3D Painter

**Main Info:**

Twisted Chess Game is an in-progress 3D chess game I built in Unity for my sixth-semester university project. It introduces the Monk, a custom chess piece with its own functional movement and gameplay logic, while retaining the fundamentals of conventional chess.

The game includes improved graphics and UI, turn-based logic, legal-move highlighting, special-move implementations, and dynamic 3D piece placement based on array coordinates. I used Blender and Substance 3D Painter for its 3D art and visual presentation.

The game is playable, but still has bugs and does not yet have mouse controls; its current interactions are driven through code. Further development is focused on stability and refining its systems.

## 13. PolyCrate

**Title:** PolyCrate  
**Short Info:** A collaborative, browser-based 3D workspace for presenting, exploring, discussing, critiquing, and annotating 3D models with up to 16 people in one session.  
**Year:** 2026 (Beta)  
**Software Used:** JavaScript

**Main Info:**

PolyCrate is a collaborative 3D workspace I have been building to make working with 3D models simpler, more interactive, and more social. Users can load a model, invite others into the same session, and present, discuss, critique, annotate, draw on, and explore the model together.

The workspace supports GLB, FBX, OBJ, and STL files, as well as materials, animations, morph targets, and level-of-detail (LOD) workflows. Sessions support up to 16 members, with Host, Presenter, Critic, and Guest roles. Interactive Memo and Doodle modes help keep feedback and ideas directly within the 3D workspace, while its clean, visual-first interface keeps attention on the model.

PolyCrate is currently in beta. The goal is not simply to provide another place to view 3D models, but to create a shared space where people can work with them together.
