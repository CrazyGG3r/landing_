Project ZAMAN — Design Overview

Project ZAMAN — Design Overview

The Oscillation of Eras

A Soulslike set in an early 20th century world fractured by the misuse of the soul.

Engine: Unreal Engine 5/6 · New Input System

Genre: 3D Action / Adventure · Soulslike · Semi-OpenWorld

Aesthetic Inspirations: Lies of P, Bloodborne

Mechanics Inspirations: Lies of P, Black Myth Wukong, Sekiro

Synopsis

A Masonic cult misidentifies Immaculate Thought — the soul’s capacity to process meaning without contradiction at extreme speed — as time manipulation. Repeated exploitation shatters the soul, triggering Soul Fission and an irreversible desynchronization of reality: the Oscillation of Eras. The protagonist, an unnamed orphan bound in a double straitjacket, must navigate a broken world where restoration is impossible and adaptation is the only path forward.

World Lore

  • Ashbourne Children’s Home — Secretly owned by the cult as a capability pipeline.
  • The Desert — The MC’s psychological landscape.
  • The Hope Entity — The MC’s childhood self. Reconnection is not a return to innocence, but a reconciliation with the self.
  • The 11th Chamber — Contains Alina’s preserved corpse. Its unveiling caused both of the MC’s soul-forms to fluctuate at the same time, triggering the Oscillation.
  • Subject-002 (The Instrument) — Post-fission entity that keeps precision and clarity but lacks empathy and creativity. Hums a distorted lullaby. Shimmering skin with sigil markings. Eventual ally to the MC.

Core Principles

01 — Naming Framework
Arabic is used for core metaphysical truths, world concepts, and protagonist-facing ideas. Hebrew is used for the cult, its rituals, subjects, and esoteric terminology. The protagonist is named in neither, which reinforces his outsider status.

02 — The Irreversibility Principle
Nothing is restored. Only adaptation is possible. The Oscillation is permanent, and the world cannot be reset or saved in the conventional sense.

03 — Hope as Integration
The game’s central theme is adaptation, not restoration. Hope is understood as the integration of a fragmented self — not salvation, not escape.

04 — Dual Identity
The MC’s two forms are not good against evil in a simple binary. The shadow form is driven by vacancy, the original by empathy. The tension between them is the game’s internal drama.

Project ZAMAN is an original Soulslike in active development.
