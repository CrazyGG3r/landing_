Ideas Inbox

Ideas Inbox

Tags: project-zaman creative creative ideas open

Purpose of this folder

Unfiltered capture. Nothing here is canon, and nothing here needs to be finished or defended. When an idea is accepted it moves to Lore/, World/, or Production/ and gets removed from this list.

Keeping this folder separate is what lets the rest of the vault stay confirmed.

How to Use This

Three states, marked inline so nothing gets mistaken for settled lore:

  • #idea — raw, unexamined
  • #considering — being weighed, may conflict with existing notes
  • #rejected — kept on purpose, with the reason, so it does not get re-proposed in six months

Rejected ideas are worth more than they look. Half the value of a long project is remembering why you said no.

Open Threads Worth Thinking About

These come from questions the vault has already raised, not from anywhere new. They are prompts, not proposals.

On the ending — Open Gaps #08 is the largest gap in the project. Worth capturing several incompatible endings here before committing to one, since the Irreversibility Principle rules out most conventional resolutions by design.

On Subject-002 — currently only ever seen at a distance. What would it cost, narratively, for that to change once? Whatever the answer, it should be expensive.

On Noam — Open Gaps #08 asks whether he is defeated, redeemed, or simply left behind. Themes argues the redemption question may no longer be meaningful, which is a harder and more interesting position than either defeat or redemption.

On the Hope Entity — it is explicitly not a cure. The Hope Entity warns that treating it as one contradicts the central metaphysical rule. Any idea that makes reconnection fix something should be checked against that.

On the factions — the Rebuild the Oasis path in The Oasis requires the player to prove both sides hold incomplete halves of the same answer. That structure could apply elsewhere in the game.

On the third miracle — silent connection across distance is the only ability that reaches other people. It is used twice and then never again in the current outline. It is the most underused idea in the vault.

Do not let this folder leak

Everything in Lore/ and World/ should be treated as confirmed. Everything here should not. If an idea is promoted, delete it from this note when you move it, so it never exists in two states at once.
