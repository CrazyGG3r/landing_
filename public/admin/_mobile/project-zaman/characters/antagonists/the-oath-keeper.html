The Oath-Keeper

Tags: project-zaman characters character antagonist overseer anthropos axios gibborim cult doctrine established

Function

The oath-keeper is the dedicated head figure who keeps Axios procedure and Gibborim doctrine aligned.

Overview

The Oath-Keeper is a senior cult authority who looks after the relationship between Axios and Gibborim.

He is not defined as a scientist alone, a priest alone, or a member of the Head Family. His role is more useful as a bridge of command: he understands the clinical work well enough to direct it, and he understands Gibborim’s doctrine well enough to enforce its purpose.

Where Axios records and Gibborim claims, the oath-keeper decides how both branches remain obedient to the same system.

Known Scene Function

After Alyna’s death and the original MC’s collapse, the oath-keeper returns with facility reinforcements.

He evaluates the scene with controlled urgency:

  • The original MC is in critical condition.
  • Alyna is dead.
  • Noam is laughing uncontrollably after direct exposure to fragmented time.

He gives quiet disposal orders for Alyna and urgent medical instructions for the original MC.

When Noam is dragged before him, the oath-keeper uses the one threat that immediately cuts through Noam’s hysteria: if Noam’s Father hears what happened, Noam’s succession can be delayed by another ten years.

Authority

The oath-keeper’s authority is practical, disciplinary, and procedural.

He does not need to be the highest political figure in Anthropos to matter. He is dangerous because he is present where doctrine becomes action: laboratories, chambers, recovery teams, disposal orders, succession discipline, and the handling of catastrophic failure.

Graph Connections

  • Anthropos gives him the public structure he serves.
  • Axios gives him the technical machinery he supervises.
  • Gibborim gives him the doctrine he enforces.
  • Head Family gives him leverage over Noam without making him part of the bloodline.
  • Noam reveals his disciplinary power.
