The Founder of the Oasis

Tags: project-zaman characters character founder father-figure oasis survivors survival deceased

Current Boundary

This page records the founder’s confirmed role without overdefining his name, politics, or personal history.

Overview

The Founder of the Oasis was the first father figure of The Oasis and one of the main reasons the early faction remained united.

He had strong Arab descent, giving the faction’s origin a human and cultural root rather than leaving it as a purely symbolic organization.

His importance does not depend on being flawless. He matters because the survivors understood him as a stabilizing presence: someone who made the original oath feel personal, practical, and worth preserving.

Legacy

After his death, The Oasis lost more than a leader.

It lost the person who had embodied the faction’s shared promise. Without him, the same oath split into two competing interpretations:

  • The Order of the Oasis claimed discipline, protection, and direct continuity with his original duty.
  • The Mirage rejected the belief that protection alone could fulfill his purpose after the world had already changed.

Both child factions speak from grief. Both claim part of his legacy. Neither fully replaces what he held together.

Writing Boundary

Keep his page restrained until the story gives him more direct scenes.

For now, his strongest use is as the emotional origin of The Oasis: the father figure whose absence made the schism possible.
