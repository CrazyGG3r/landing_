Head Family

Tags: project-zaman factions faction index authority anthropos head-family cult partial

Purpose

This note keeps the known Anthropos family line focused. It should only contain confirmed or strongly implied members.

Overview

The Head Family is the dynastic human face attached to Anthropos and its hidden authority structure.

It should not be treated as a large visible roster yet. At this stage, only two necessary figures are dedicated enough for their own pages:

MemberRoleCurrent detail level
NoamActive heir and mirror antagonistEstablished
Noam’s FatherBedridden patriarch and inheritance pressureMentioned

The Head Family gives Noam’s position more weight than ordinary membership. He is not only a trained believer within Gibborim; he belongs to a family line whose approval, succession, and punishment can shape his future inside the cult.

Relationship to the Oath-Keeper

The Oath-Keeper is not listed as a confirmed Head Family member.

His importance is operational rather than dynastic. He oversees the alignment between Axios and Gibborim, and he has enough authority to threaten Noam’s succession by invoking Noam’s Father.

This creates a useful separation:

  • The Head Family carries inheritance, bloodline, and political pressure.
  • The Oath-Keeper carries active command, discipline, and cult procedure.

Writing Boundary

Do not add extra relatives unless the story explicitly needs them.

The Head Family works best as a small pressure system around Noam: a father above him, an inheritance ahead of him, and an overseer who can expose his failure.
