Axios

Tags: project-zaman factions faction child-faction overseen axios cult science established

“The Science Division”

The cult’s analytical engine. It does not ask whether something should be done, only how precisely it can be observed, measured, and repeated.

Origin: Greek, áxios, meaning “worthy”
Parent: Anthropos
Layer: Public-facing scientific division
Private Counterpart: None
Operational Overseer: The Oath-Keeper
Symbol: A black serpent, depicted without hook, fang, or ornament

Overview

Axios is the scientific and empirical division of the cult, operating publicly beneath the Anthropos banner as a legitimate research institution.

Its name frames measurement as worth: what can be observed, repeated, and archived becomes worthy of attention. What cannot be measured is either ignored or forced into a form Axios can record.

Unlike the other branches, Axios possesses no concealed identity. Its purpose remains unchanged across both the public and inner layers. It observes, classifies, documents, and systematizes every phenomenon the cult encounters, including individuals with rare cognitive or soul-based abilities.

Axios provides the intellectual credibility displayed by Anthropos and the technical foundation required by Gibborim. Under the oath-keeper’s oversight, Axios determines what subjects are, how they function, and how they may be reproduced or controlled.

Primary Functions

  • Conducts research into soul-based phenomena, including Immaculate Thought and its effects on the human mind, body, and soul.

  • Develops and maintains the cult’s subject classification, identification, and numbering systems.

  • Records Soul Fission, the Oscillation of Eras, and all observable consequences caused by the misuse of Immaculate Thought.

  • Designs, operates, and maintains experimental chambers, containment systems, preservation equipment, and monitoring instruments.

  • Oversees the technical handling of subjects before, during, and after experimentation.

  • Produces clinical reports and findings for Gibborim while withholding sensitive material from the broader Anthropos institution.

  • Archives failed procedures, abnormal outcomes, and contradictions without moral interpretation.

Institutional Character

Axios is the most quietly dangerous of the cult’s divisions because it possesses no true ideology of its own.

Its members are scientists, physicians, archivists, mathematicians, and engineers who have separated empirical procedure from moral consequence. They do not consider themselves executioners or zealots. They consider themselves observers.

The horror of Axios lies in its neutrality.

Axios would document the collapse of a human soul with the same composure used to record a pulse, temperature, or mechanical fault. It neither celebrates suffering nor condemns it. Every scream becomes data. Every death becomes a recorded outcome. Every failure becomes the foundation of a more precise experiment.

It does not desire catastrophe.

It simply refuses to stop measuring it.

Where Anthropos smiles and Gibborim commands, Axios records.

Naming and Language

Axios has no Hebrew inner name because it belongs entirely to the cult’s public and scientific register. Its terminology remains clinical, procedural, and deliberately stripped of spiritual implication.

Its reports avoid ritual language, even when documenting metaphysical events. Souls become measurable structures. Gifts become cognitive anomalies. Human beings become subjects, specimens, and numbered cases.

The subject identification system, including designations such as Subject-001 and Subject-002, originated within Axios’ archives before being adopted and reinterpreted by Gibborim within its esoteric hierarchy.

Symbol

Axios is represented by a black serpent.

Unlike traditional serpent imagery, it bears no hook, crown, open mouth, or visible fang. It is shown as silent and controlled, suggesting observation, precision, continuity, and knowledge without declared intent.

The serpent does not strike.

It watches.

It did not build the threshold. It measured its exact dimensions.
