“In Service of All Mankind”

The cult’s public face: a humanitarian mask concealing a system of exploitation.

Origin: Greek, ánthrōpos, meaning “human being”
Layer: Parent institution; public-facing cult structure
Public Symbol: None
Children: Axios, Gibborim
Known family authority: Head Family

Overview

Anthropos presents itself as a philanthropic and intellectual order devoted to the advancement of humanity. It operates openly through legitimate institutions, including orphanages, academic sponsorships, medical initiatives, and civic development programs.

Behind this respectable exterior, Anthropos serves as the recruitment, observation, and acquisition arm of the cult’s inner order. It identifies individuals with unusual cognitive, spiritual, or metaphysical potential, then quietly places them within systems controlled by Gibborim.

Most members of Anthropos are unaware that Gibborim even exists.

Hierarchy

Anthropos is the parent structure used to organize the cult’s public and hidden branches.

BranchRoleVisibility
AxiosScientific division; observes, classifies, and records soul-based phenomenaPublic-facing under Anthropos, but with restricted findings
GibborimPrivate esoteric order; assigns doctrine, ownership, and ultimate purposeHidden from most Anthropos members

In-world, this hierarchy is deceptive. Anthropos appears to be the parent authority, but Gibborim secretly directs the system from within. Axios functions as the technical child branch that gives Anthropos credibility while serving Gibborim’s deeper agenda.

Known Authority and Members

Anthropos should not receive a large member roster until the story clearly needs one. Its current authority structure works best when kept focused:

Figure or groupRole
Head FamilyThe dynastic family line attached to Anthropos authority; currently centered on Noam and Noam’s Father.
The Oath-KeeperThe active overseer who keeps Axios procedure and Gibborim doctrine aligned.
NoamThe active heir and mirror antagonist shaped by the cult’s ideals.

This keeps the hierarchy readable: the Head Family represents inheritance and political pressure, while the oath-keeper represents command, discipline, and operational control.

Primary Functions

  • Operates institutions such as Ashbourne Children’s Home, presenting them as welfare programs while using them to identify and cultivate valuable candidates.

  • Monitors individuals who display rare cognitive abilities, spiritual sensitivity, or unusual soul-based characteristics.

  • Transfers selected subjects into controlled environments through adoption, sponsorship, employment, medical care, or academic placement.

  • Maintains public legitimacy through charity, civic service, and intellectual patronage.

  • Redirects suspicion toward administrative failures, isolated misconduct, or unfortunate circumstances.

  • Acts as a protective outer layer, ensuring that public scrutiny never reaches Gibborim.

Institutional Character

Anthropos is polished, disciplined, and idealistic in its presentation. Its rhetoric revolves around human potential, collective progress, moral responsibility, and enlightened stewardship.

To the public, it resembles a progressive philanthropic order composed of scholars, patrons, physicians, educators, and civic leaders. Its members speak sincerely of improving society and guiding humanity toward a more prosperous future.

That sincerity is what makes Anthropos dangerous.

Most of its operatives are not sadists, conspirators, or willing servants of evil. They genuinely believe their work benefits humanity. They see vulnerable children as lives to be guided, gifted individuals as talents to be cultivated, and sacrifice as an unfortunate necessity of progress.

They do not know what waits beyond the threshold.

The smile that conceals the threshold.
