Tags: project-zaman index home active

Vault Home

Project ZAMAN is a dark metaphysical tragedy about a time-gifted orphan, a cult that turns care and science into instruments of exploitation, and a broken world where hope means integration rather than restoration.

Editorial Rules

  • Preserve the lore. Improve structure, clarity, grammar, and consistency without changing established events.
  • Use Alyna as the character’s name everywhere.
  • Use Axios as the cult’s scientific child branch under Anthropos.
  • Keep restoration impossible. The Irreversibility Principle is the project’s spine.
  • Treat open questions as deliberate design space, not errors.
