Faction Territory Split

Useful when placing encounters — The Oasis already divides the map by ideology:

  • Order of the Oasis — settlements, supply routes, defensive outposts, resource-gathering areas, former Anthropos bases
  • The Mirage — cult facilities, abandoned laboratories, ritual sites, temporally distorted regions, areas under strong cult influence

That division does most of the work of a world map already. A player travelling toward the truth moves geographically away from safety.

Open Questions

  • Is the facility the main playable space? It is the emotional center of the entire backstory and is described as still standing in distorted form. Nothing yet says whether the player can enter it.
  • Are the mansion and the facility the same site? Story Structure has Alyna working as a garden worker “at the mansion” while the MC is held in “the facility,” and the garden is where her tombstone later appears. They read as one complex, but this is never stated.
  • Is the Desert enterable? Whether it is a playable area, a cutscene space, or a recurring interstitial changes a great deal about the game’s structure.
  • What is a “temporally distorted region” to walk through? Named in The Oasis as Mirage territory, never described.
  • Does Ashbourne still exist after the Oscillation? It survived the fire. Its post-Catastrophe state would be an obvious and powerful early location.
  • Where is the founder of The Oasis buried, or lost? A pilgrimage site both successor factions claim would give the schism a physical anchor.

Level design hook

Two locations in this project already carry the whole theme without needing dialogue: an empty orphanage the children keep returning to, and a garden where a grave now stands where a kiss once happened. Both are places whose meaning changed without the place itself changing.
