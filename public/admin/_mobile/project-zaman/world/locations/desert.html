”Every grain is a moment loosened from its place.”

Type: Psychological landscape
Era: Before and after the Oscillation, inwardly
Belongs to: Subject-001
Related: The Hope Entity, MC, Irreversibility Principle

Overview

The Desert is Subject-001’s inner psychological landscape, inherited from the original MC.

It first appears as an endless space where the MC crawls toward a glowing figure. Later, the image is understood as the sand inside an hourglass and as the internal path toward The Hope Entity.

Behavior

The Desert is not geographically stable. Distance, weather, light, and scale shift according to Subject-001’s coherence.

The Hope Entity does not move through the Desert. Instead, Subject-001’s ability to perceive and approach it changes.

Meaning

The Desert is where the project turns the theme of time into an image of trauma.

It does not offer escape from the broken world. It offers the possibility of integration within it.
