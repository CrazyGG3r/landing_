Tags: project-zaman world timeline timeline working

Purpose of this folder

The order of events, anchored to the MC’s age wherever the vault gives one. There is no calendar in Project ZAMAN yet — no year, no dates — so this timeline is relative. Individual events belong in Lore/Events; this note only fixes their sequence.

Open Questions

  • The seventeen-year problem. Story Structure says “seventeen years of captivity” three times, but he is seventeen at the escape and was extracted as a child. Either the phrase means his whole life, or the number needs changing. Nothing else in the timeline depends on it, so it is safe to leave — but it will confuse a reader.
  • How long between the Oscillation and the playable present? Long enough for a faction to form, lose a founder, and split. That is years, not months, but no figure is given.
  • When does the Fabrication Demise happen? Simultaneous with the Oscillation, or a slower aftermath? The Order’s denial reads very differently depending on the answer.
  • How long was the reunion period? It is deferred, but its length affects Alyna’s age and the MC’s at the recapture.
  • Is a calendar wanted at all? An unplaced, undated world suits the themes. But a Soulslike usually needs some era markers for its item descriptions and environmental storytelling.

Ages that are fixed

Alyna is four years older than the MC. He is seventeen at the first escape, fourteen at the third miracle, and she began her campaign at eighteen. Everything else is relative.
