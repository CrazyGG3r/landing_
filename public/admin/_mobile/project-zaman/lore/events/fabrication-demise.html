Fabrication Demise

Tags: project-zaman lore event catastrophe population-loss undefined

”The loss no faction can prove, and no honest protector can ignore.”

When: After or during the Oscillation of Eras; exact timing unresolved
Where: Across the post-Catastrophe world
Status: Confirmed, undefined
Related: The Oasis, The Order of the Oasis, The Mirage, Irreversibility Principle

What Is Known

The Fabrication Demise is the widespread disappearance or loss of a vast portion of humanity after the Catastrophe.

Their deaths are not fully confirmed.

This uncertainty becomes the moral fault line between The Order of the Oasis and The Mirage.

Faction Meaning

The Order focuses on confirmed survivors and treats the unverified population as beyond practical reach.

The Mirage sees that refusal as a betrayal of humanity’s wider fate and searches dangerous regions for answers.

Neither side has solved the problem.

Open Questions

  • Were the missing people killed, displaced, desynchronized, transformed, trapped, or preserved in another state?
  • Did the Fabrication Demise happen at the same moment as the Oscillation, or unfold afterward?
  • Can any part of this population still be found or helped?
