Oscillation of Eras

Oscillation of Eras

Tags: project-zaman lore event catastrophe time irreversibility established

”The world did not rewind. It came apart and stayed that way.”

When: Final catastrophe before the post-Oscillation era
Where: The cult facility
Participants: MC, Noam, Axios, Gibborim
Status: Established

What Happens

The Oscillation of Eras occurs when the original MC’s opposing soul-forms destabilize at the same time under maximum experimental pressure.

Noam orders the procedure to continue despite warnings. The contradiction inside the MC’s soul becomes metaphysically real, reality desynchronizes, and different historical eras begin colliding in the same world.

Cause

The immediate trigger is the reveal of the Eleventh Chamber, where Alyna’s preserved body is used against the MC.

The deeper cause is the cult’s long misuse of Immaculate Thought, its extraction systems, and Noam’s refusal to accept failure.

Consequence

  • The original MC fractures into Subject-001 and Subject-002.
  • The facility becomes a decayed temporal ruin.
  • On-site cult personnel die.
  • Reality remains permanently desynchronized.
  • The Irreversibility Principle becomes unavoidable.
  • The survivor era begins, eventually leading to The Oasis.

What It Is Not

The Oscillation is not a reset, a cycle, or a repairable timeline error.

It is the permanent state the rest of the story must inhabit.
