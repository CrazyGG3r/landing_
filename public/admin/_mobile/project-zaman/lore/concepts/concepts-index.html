Purpose of this folder

One note per metaphysical or philosophical concept in Project ZAMAN. Concepts are the rules of the world: what a thing is and why it behaves as it does.

Keep events in Lore/Events and systems the player interacts with in Lore/Mechanics. A concept explains; a mechanic operates.

Open Questions

  • What does “Zaman” name? The Project Zaman naming framework reserves Arabic for core metaphysical truths and protagonist-facing ideas, but no Arabic term is currently defined anywhere in the vault. Is Zaman a concept characters can speak of in-world, or only the project’s title?
  • Is there an Arabic vocabulary at all yet? The framework promises one. At present Hebrew (Gibborim) and Greek (Anthropos, Axios) are the only languages with actual terms behind them. This is the largest unfilled gap in the naming system.
  • What exactly is the Fabrication Demise? The Oasis establishes its consequences — an unaccounted-for population, and a moral fault line between the two successor factions — but not its nature. Were people destroyed, displaced, desynchronized, or something else? The Order’s denial only works dramatically if the answer is genuinely uncertain in-world.
  • Is temporal desynchronization one condition or several? Colliding eras, distorted regions, and blind spots in perception are all described. Are these the same phenomenon at different scales?
  • What are time fragments, materially? They exist as prototype samples and as an extractable resource. Whether they are objects, energy, or something else is not stated.

Rule of thumb

If a concept can be misunderstood by the cult, record both the true version and the version they teach. That gap is where most of this project’s tension lives.
