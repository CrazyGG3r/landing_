Irreversibility Principle

Irreversibility Principle

Tags: project-zaman lore concept irreversibility adaptation theme established

”Nothing is restored. What remains must learn how to continue.”

Category: Metaphysical and thematic law
Status: Established
Related: Themes, Open Gaps, Oscillation of Eras, The Hope Entity, The Oasis

Definition

The Irreversibility Principle states that the damage caused by the Oscillation of Eras cannot be undone.

Time cannot be restored. Alyna cannot return. The original MC cannot become whole again. The pre-Oscillation world is not waiting to be recovered.

Narrative Function

This principle protects the core theme of Project ZAMAN:

Hope is integration, not restoration.

The story is not about reversing trauma. It is about continuing after irreversible damage without allowing that damage to become the whole identity.

Faction Relevance

Gibborim treats the principle as doctrine and plans around permanence.

The Oasis and its child factions live inside the consequences of the principle, even when they disagree over whether survival depends on protection, knowledge, or reunification.
