Soul Fission

Soul Fission

Tags: project-zaman lore concept soul fission identity established

”One person divided into consequences.”

Category: Metaphysical rupture
Status: Established
Related: MC, Subject-001, Subject-002, The Hope Entity, Oscillation of Eras

Definition

Soul Fission is the division of one unified existence into incomplete fragments under intolerable contradiction.

In Project ZAMAN, it occurs when the original MC’s opposing soul-forms destabilize during the Oscillation of Eras.

Result

Soul Fission creates:

  • Subject-001, who carries the clearest emotional and moral continuity.
  • Subject-002, who carries the original body, the straitjacket, and physical trauma.
  • The Hope Entity, which remains as the preserved childhood self inside the Desert.

The division is not clean. Each fragment carries traces that complicate any simple claim to being the original MC.

What It Is Not

Soul Fission is not cloning, resurrection, reincarnation, or restoration.

It is a catastrophic distribution of body, soul, memory, trauma, and identity after the original unity can no longer survive.
