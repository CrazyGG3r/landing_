Shadow Form

Shadow Form

Tags: project-zaman lore mechanic shadow trauma mechanics unresolved

”The self that suffering built to survive without tenderness.”

Type: Metaphysical and psychological state
Belongs to: MC; inherited in fragments by Subject-001 and Subject-002
Status: Established, mechanics unresolved
Related: Alyna, Noam, Oscillation of Eras, The Hope Entity

Definition

The Shadow Form is the darker defensive state produced inside the original MC through captivity, vacancy, experimentation, and grief.

It is not a separate complete soul before the fission. It is one of the two unstable soul-forms inside the same person.

Function

The Shadow Form can act with violence, efficiency, and detachment when the original MC’s ordinary self cannot endure what is happening.

Its power is dangerous because it offers protection at the cost of moral continuity.

Alyna’s Warning

Before her death, Alyna warns the MC not to let the Shadow Form overtake him.

He hears the warning, understands it, and still chooses the shadow after her death. That contradiction becomes one of the defining wounds inherited by Subject-001.

Open Design Question

Whether the Shadow Form can be resisted, redirected, accelerated, or expressed as a player-facing mechanic remains unresolved in Open Gaps.
