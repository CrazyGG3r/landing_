Open Gaps

Open Gaps

Tags: project-zaman narrative narrative tracker open

These are the structural and creative questions that Project ZAMAN has not yet resolved.

They are not failures or missing pieces. They are deliberate spaces left open for decisions that will define the ending, the player’s agency, and the final emotional character of the world.

01. The True Name of the Original MC

The Original MC exists outside the Hebrew naming system imposed by Anthropos. Within the cult’s records, Axios reduces him to a numerical designation rather than recognising him as a person.

His true name has never appeared in the narrative.

It remains undecided whether the name will eventually be revealed, permanently withheld, or reserved as the final gesture of the story.

Why It Matters

The name represents more than character identity. It determines whether the system ever succeeded in fully containing and redefining him.

  • A revealed name becomes an act of reclamation.

  • A permanently withheld name suggests that identity does not require institutional recognition.

  • A name spoken only at the end could become the final confirmation that something of the original person survived.

Status: Undecided.

02. The Miracles After the Fission

The three miracles belonged to the unified soul of the Original MC:

  1. Perceptual Time Quantification

  2. Epistemological Object Contact

  3. Silent Connection Across Distance

After the Oscillation of Eras fractures him into Subject-001 and Subject-002, the fate of these miracles becomes uncertain.

What is established is that Subject-001 does not inherit them.

Possibilities Under Consideration

  • The miracles become dormant and belong to neither fragment.

  • They manifest unpredictably without being controlled by either subject.

  • They disappear completely with the unified soul that produced them.

  • Their remnants survive only through locations, memories, or people previously affected by them.

Why It Matters

The fate of the miracles determines whether the fission divided only the person or also destroyed the metaphysical conditions that made his abilities possible.

It also affects whether integration can produce something new without falsely restoring what was lost.

Status: Undecided. Deferred until the narrative mechanics are more clearly defined.

03. The Relationship Between Subject-001 and Subject-002

Subject-001 initially perceives Subject-002 only as a distant mystery: a restrained figure glimpsed at the edge of perception, separated from him by space, memory, and incomplete understanding.

What their relationship becomes over the course of the narrative remains unresolved.

Possible Directions

  • They gradually move toward one another.

  • They come into direct ideological or physical conflict.

  • They influence each other without ever meeting consciously.

  • They remain permanently parallel, each sensing the absence represented by the other.

  • One fragment attempts to absorb, replace, or erase the other.

  • They reach recognition without achieving reunification.

Why It Matters

Their relationship is the most direct consequence of the soul fission.

It determines what integration means within the world:

  • Reconnection

  • Mutual recognition

  • Coexistence

  • Domination

  • Acceptance of permanent separation

It also decides whether the Original MC can be considered restored in any meaningful sense, even if literal restoration remains impossible.

Status: Undecided.

04. Hope Entity Reconnection

The Hope Entity is the preserved childhood self of the Original MC, existing within the Desert.

Subject-001 must confront or reconnect with it as part of his movement toward integration. The exact nature of this process has not yet been established.

Outstanding Questions

  • Is the reconnection a single decisive event or a gradual process across the narrative?

  • Does Subject-001 experience it as memory, confrontation, recognition, dissolution, or something else?

  • Does the Hope Entity willingly accept him?

  • Does Subject-001 understand what the entity represents before reaching it?

  • Does the reconnection alter him physically, mechanically, emotionally, or only internally?

  • Can the player delay, damage, or fail the process?

  • Does reconnection preserve the Hope Entity or cause it to disappear into Subject-001?

  • Is the result integration, coexistence, or inheritance?

Why It Matters

The Hope Entity is not merely a missing piece to be collected. It represents the part of the original soul that still believes existence can lead somewhere other than suffering.

How Subject-001 approaches it will determine whether integration is treated as recovery, acceptance, sacrifice, or transformation.

Status: Undecided.

05. Alyna’s Post-Mortem Fate

Alyna is killed before the Oscillation of Eras fully resolves.

It remains uncertain what death means inside the fractured post-Oscillation world, and whether any part of her remains accessible afterward.

Possibilities Under Consideration

  • Alyna is completely absent.

  • Fragments of her memories remain embedded in Subject-001.

  • Temporal echoes of her exist without consciousness.

  • Her presence survives only through environments altered by the Oscillation.

  • Something resembling Alyna appears, but cannot be confirmed as truly being her.

  • She survives only through the final instruction she gave the Original MC.

Why It Matters

Alyna’s bond with the Original MC is the emotional axis of the narrative.

If something of her survives, the story must determine whether that survival offers genuine connection or merely prolongs grief.

If nothing remains, then the Irreversibility Principle applies to her without exception.

Her fate therefore determines whether the narrative allows consolation, ambiguity, or absolute loss.

Status: Undecided.

06. The Cult’s Internal Hierarchy

The internal structure of Gibborim has not yet been fully mapped.

The Hebrew naming convention is established, but the titles, responsibilities, ranks, and individual figures within the organisation remain incomplete.

Areas Still Requiring Definition

  • The supreme authority of the cult

  • The relationship between spiritual and scientific leadership

  • The individuals responsible for subject acquisition

  • The figures overseeing experimentation

  • The keepers of historical and metaphysical knowledge

  • The internal enforcement body

  • The people responsible for public institutions such as Ashbourne Children’s Home

  • The level of knowledge held by lower-ranking members

  • Whether the highest ranks understand the true nature of the Oscillation

  • Whether internal factions disagree over the interpretation of the miracles

Why It Matters

The hierarchy determines whether Gibborim functions as a unified order or as a collection of competing interpretations hidden beneath one doctrine.

It also defines how responsibility is distributed. The catastrophe may have been caused by the cult as a whole, by a small inner circle, or by individuals manipulating the institution for separate purposes.

Status: Partially undecided. Naming, ranks, and individual roles remain deferred.

07. Player Agency and Branching

The extent to which player decisions reshape the narrative has not yet been determined.

The central question is whether the player changes the events themselves or primarily changes how Subject-001 understands and responds to them.

Potential Branching Points

  • Whether Subject-001’s discovery of his origin can be delayed or accelerated

  • Whether important memories can be missed, rejected, or misinterpreted

  • Whether the pull toward the Shadow Form can be resisted, embraced, or redirected

  • Whether the Hope Entity reconnection is mandatory

  • Whether Subject-002 can be approached differently depending on previous choices

  • Whether Noam’s fate is determined by confrontation, mercy, abandonment, or refusal

  • Whether the player shapes the ending itself or only its emotional tone

Structural Models Under Consideration

Fixed Narrative

The major events remain unchanged, while player choices affect dialogue, interpretation, and emotional context.

Limited Branching

Several major decisions alter relationships, encounters, and the tone of the ending without changing the central conclusion.

Multiple Conclusions

The player’s choices significantly alter Subject-001’s final state, Noam’s fate, and the condition of the post-Oscillation world.

Why It Matters

Too little agency may make the player feel detached from Subject-001’s internal struggle.

Too much agency may weaken the project’s established themes, particularly the Irreversibility Principle and the impossibility of complete restoration.

Status: Undecided. Structural decisions pending.

08. The Final Narrative

The ending of Project ZAMAN remains the largest unresolved part of the project.

It must determine the final state of Subject-001, the fate of Subject-002, the consequence of the Hope Entity reconnection, the conclusion of Noam’s arc, and the condition of the world after the Oscillation.

What Is Established

  • Complete restoration is impossible.

  • The Irreversibility Principle remains absolute.

  • The ending must center on adaptation rather than triumph.

  • Alyna’s final instruction remains Subject-001’s moral compass.

  • The Original MC cannot simply return as though the fission never occurred.

  • Any form of integration must preserve the consequences of what happened.

What Remains Unresolved

  • Whether Subject-001 fully reconnects with the Hope Entity

  • Whether Subject-001 and Subject-002 ever meet directly

  • Whether either fragment survives the ending unchanged

  • Whether Noam is defeated, redeemed, forgiven, abandoned, or left unresolved

  • Whether Gibborim survives the collapse of its doctrine

  • Whether the post-Oscillation world remains fractured

  • Whether the world stabilizes into a different form of existence

  • Whether Alyna leaves behind an echo or only a memory

  • Whether the Original MC’s true name is ever spoken

  • Whether the final moment belongs to Subject-001, Subject-002, the player, or the absent Original MC

Intended Emotional Direction

The ending should not feel like victory over the past.

It should feel like a decision to continue despite the fact that the past cannot be repaired.

The final state of the world may remain wounded, but the narrative must establish whether that wound becomes a prison, an identity, or something its survivors learn to carry.

Status: Undecided. The ending will be written only after the project’s central relationships and mechanics are firmly established.

Open gaps are not absences.

They are the spaces in which the project’s most important decisions are still allowed to breathe.
