Keywords

Keywords

Tags: project-zaman home reference index active

Purpose

A concise glossary of recurring Project ZAMAN terms. Full explanations remain in the linked notes.

Core Lore

KeywordMeaning
Project ZAMANThe complete setting and narrative centered on the Original MC, the Oscillation, and the fractured world that follows. See Project Zaman.
Original MCThe unnamed, time-connected protagonist exploited by Anthropos before being divided during the Oscillation. See MC.
Immaculate ThoughtAn extreme state of cognition expressed through the Original MC’s three miracles. It is not ordinary time manipulation. See Immaculate Thought.
Oscillation of ErasThe catastrophe that desynchronized reality and caused incompatible eras to collide. Also called the Catastrophe. See Oscillation of Eras.
Soul FissionThe division of the Original MC into incomplete continuations under intolerable metaphysical contradiction. See Soul Fission.
Time ShardsBright, single-use crystals containing violently ticking temporal fragments. They can retrieve possibilities from other temporal realities. See Time Shards.
Irreversibility PrincipleThe rule that this world’s losses cannot be undone by replacing them with alternatives. See Irreversibility Principle.
Fabrication DemiseThe widespread and still-unexplained loss of people after the Catastrophe. See Fabrication Demise.
Era of RequiemGibborim’s promised New World Order, built on control of Time Shards and forbidden possibilities. See Era of Requiem.

People and Fragments

KeywordMeaning
Subject-001The soul-dominant playable continuation of the Original MC. He seeks the Time Shards and his complete memories. See Subject-001.
Subject-002The body-dominant continuation carrying the Original MC’s body, physical trauma, and conditioned responses. See Subject-002.
Hope EntityThe rarely encountered preservation of the Original MC’s childhood self within the Desert. See The Hope Entity.
Shadow FormA defensive soul-state shaped by trauma, vacancy, rage, and the rejection of empathy. See Shadow Form.
AlynaThe Original MC’s caretaker, chosen soulmate, intended future wife, emotional anchor, and enduring moral influence. See Alyna.
NoamAnthropos heir, childhood observer, and central antagonist whose choices help trigger the Oscillation. See Noam.
BeethovenA blind symphony fighter, recurring boss assistant, and possible opponent drawn toward a Time Shard. See Beethoven.
MozartBeethoven’s true mentor, whose warning and betrayal provide the moral context for Beethoven’s ambition. See Mozart.

Factions and Institutions

KeywordMeaning
AnthroposThe cult’s parent institution and respectable humanitarian mask. See Anthropos.
AxiosAnthropos’s scientific child branch, responsible for observation, classification, and experimentation. See Axios.
GibborimAnthropos’s hidden doctrinal child branch and the true authority behind the cult. See Gibborim.
The OasisA survivor parent faction founded after the Catastrophe. See The Oasis.
Order of the OasisThe protective child faction focused on confirmed survivors, territory, and supplies. See The Order of the Oasis.
The MirageThe exploratory child faction seeking truth about the Catastrophe and the missing population. See The Mirage.

Places and Symbols

KeywordMeaning
The DesertThe inner psychological and metaphysical landscape inherited by Subject-001. See Desert.
Ashbourne Children’s HomeThe orphanage used by Anthropos to observe and acquire vulnerable children. See Ashbourne Children’s Home.
Eleventh ChamberThe chamber containing Alyna’s preserved body and used to force the Original MC beyond his final limit. See Eleventh Chamber.
HourglassThe central image of time, enclosure, memory, and the Desert’s true scale. See Desert and MC.

Player Systems and Outcomes

KeywordMeaning
Honor SystemMoral progression that shapes Subject-001’s developing identity, relationships, and available conclusions. See Honor System.
Possible EndingsThe current range of player-dependent conclusions, from voluntary integration to mutual destruction. See Possible Endings.
Musical NotesCollectibles gathered across boss attempts to qualify for Beethoven’s assistance. The exact rules remain in development. See Beethoven.

Glossary Rule

Add a keyword when it recurs across several notes or requires a precise in-world meaning. Keep character biographies, event sequences, and full mechanical rules in their dedicated notes.
