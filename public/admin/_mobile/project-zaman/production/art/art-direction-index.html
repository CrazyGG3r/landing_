Purpose of this folder

Visual direction, character and environment design, and the production pipeline for art assets. Symbolic meaning lives in Symbols/; this folder is about how things are actually made and how they look.

Established

Aesthetic references: Lies of P and Bloodborne (Project Zaman).

Period: early 20th century, which is already doing real work in the existing descriptions — vintage maid uniforms, well-made clothes kept immaculate, newspapers, an ornate letter crate, a wheelchair, laboratory chambers.

Design details already fixed in writing:

  • The MC — 188 cm, lean-muscled, bound in a double straitjacket
  • Alyna — 172 cm, vintage black-and-white maid uniform, worn continuously by choice
  • Noam — same age and build as the MC, close enough in the face to read as a distorted reflection; immaculate clothing, trained posture
  • Subject-002 — the straitjacket, seen only at a distance
  • The shadow form — eyes completely red with intensified blood flow
  • The Gibborim serpent — white, hissing mouth, anchor-hook tail
  • The Axios serpent — black, no hook, no fang, no ornament

Gaps in the Reference Set

  • Subject-002 has no reference art, despite being one of the two player-facing fragments and needing to be recognizable at long range.
  • The Hope Entity has none. It is described only as a glowing figure.
  • The Order of the Oasis and The Mirage now have working visual identities in their faction notes, but they still need tested concept sheets.
  • The Desert has none, though The Hope Entity describes its light, weather, distance, and scale as state-driven.
  • Environments generally. Every reference currently in the vault is a character or a sigil. No location art exists.

Open Questions

  • What is the silhouette rule that makes Subject-001 and Subject-002 readable as related but distinct at a glance?
  • How is a straitjacket animated for a playable or semi-playable figure without it reading as comedy?
  • What does temporal desynchronization look like in an environment — visible seams, doubled geometry, mismatched lighting, or something quieter?
  • Is the shadow form a full model swap, a material change, or a lighting effect?
  • What is the render target — stylized realism, painterly, high-contrast?
  • Where do the images actually live? _Media/_Images keeps them separate from audio, video, and PDFs, but default Pasted image <timestamp> names will not scale past a few dozen assets.
