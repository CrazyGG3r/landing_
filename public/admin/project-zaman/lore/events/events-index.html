Purpose of this folder

One note per discrete event that changes the world or a character permanently. Story Structure holds the continuous narrative; this folder holds the individual load-bearing moments, so they can be examined, cross-referenced, and revised without rewriting the whole document.

Events Needing Their Own Note First

The three highest-value ones to write out, because everything else hangs off them:

  1. **The Eleventh Chamber** — the proximate trigger of the Oscillation, currently described only inside longer documents.
  2. The Ashbourne Fire — the one event where Alyna’s network and the MC’s third miracle act in parallel without knowing about each other.
  3. **The Fabrication Demise** — the only major event with confirmed consequences and no definition. It drives the entire Order/Mirage moral conflict in The Oasis.

Open Questions

  • Two events are explicitly deferred in Story Structure: the period of reunion between the MC and Alyna, and the second escape itself. Both are marked “left for later detailed documentation.”
  • Where does the Fabrication Demise sit relative to the Oscillation? Simultaneous with it, a direct consequence, or a slower aftermath? The Oasis’s founding depends on the answer.
  • Timeline arithmetic to settle: Story Structure describes “seventeen years of captivity” three times, but the MC is seventeen years old at the escape and was extracted as a child. Either the phrase means his whole life, or the captivity figure needs changing.
  • Is the third miracle contact (age fourteen) a single event or an ongoing state? It is used again after the escape, which suggests a capability rather than a one-off.
  • How much time passes after the Oscillation? The survivor era clearly lasts years, but the exact gap before Subject-001’s playable present remains undefined.
