Purpose of this folder

One note per system that operates — something with inputs, limits, and failure states. Where Lore/Concepts explains what a thing is, this folder records how it behaves under pressure.

Gameplay implementation belongs in Production/Design. This folder stays in-fiction.

The Three Miracles

Established in MC and rooted in Immaculate Thought. All three belong to the original MC and all three require internal calm — fear, grief, anger, panic, and sensory overload interfere with them. This makes the cult’s escalating violence self-defeating, which Axios continues to ignore.

Perceptual Time QuantificationCompresses long chains of cause and effect into immediate understanding; probable outcomes become internally measurable hours aheadAccuracy falls the further ahead he reaches; strongest over short spans and familiar variables. Epistemological Object ContactAbsorbs the informational content of a book or meaningful object through touchGrants information, not comprehension. He can receive a text without the context to interpret it, or knowledge whose emotional weight exceeds what he can process
Silent ConnectionMaintains awareness of a person, place, or state of meaning across distanceNot telepathy and carries no language. The least measurable of the three, and the one Axios understands least

Open Questions

  • Do the miracles survive the fission? Open Gaps #02 settles that Subject-001 does not directly inherit them, but their broader fate remains undecided. They may be dormant, appear unpredictably, or be gone with the unified soul that generated them.
  • What is the cost of using a miracle? Nothing in the vault describes fatigue, backlash, or a price. For a Soulslike this is probably the single most important undefined mechanic.
  • Can the shadow form be resisted mechanically, or only narratively? Open Gaps #07 lists this as an open branch point.
  • What does the calm requirement mean in play? It is a beautiful constraint — abilities that switch off under exactly the conditions a boss fight creates. Whether that becomes a real system or stays fictional needs deciding.
  • Are time fragments usable by anyone other than the cult? They exist post-Oscillation as scattered material, and The Mirage recovers relics from cult sites.

Note Template

Design constraint to preserve

Every miracle in this project is an inward ability that requires stillness. None of them are exerted by force. Any new mechanic that works through aggression or willpower will contradict the premise established in MC.
